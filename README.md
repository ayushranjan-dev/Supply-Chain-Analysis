# Supply Chain Analysis 

## Project Overview

This project analyzes supply chain data for a fashion and beauty startup using Microsoft Excel. The analysis focuses on product performance, revenue, inventory, logistics costs, transportation modes, and defect rates.

The objective is to identify operational patterns and generate insights that can support supply chain decision-making.

## Objectives

- Analyze revenue across different product types
- Examine revenue generated by individual SKUs
- Analyze stock levels and order quantities
- Compare shipping carriers and associated costs
- Evaluate average lead time and manufacturing costs
- Analyze transportation cost distribution
- Examine product defect rates
- Analyze defect rates across transportation modes
- Create visualizations to communicate supply chain insights

## Key Analysis

### Product Performance

Sales were analyzed across cosmetics, haircare, and skincare categories.

Skincare accounted for approximately 45% of total sales, followed by haircare at approximately 29.5% and cosmetics at approximately 25.5%.

### Shipping Carrier Analysis

Revenue was compared across three shipping carriers.

Carrier B generated the highest revenue among the three carriers. However, it also recorded the highest shipping cost, with a total shipping cost of 237 compared with 156 for Carrier A and 162 for Carrier C.

### Lead Time & Manufacturing Cost

Average lead time and manufacturing costs were analyzed across product categories.

| Metric | Overall Average |
|---|---:|
| Lead Time | 15.96 |
| Manufacturing Cost | 47.267 |

### SKU Analysis

SKU-level analysis was performed to examine:

- Revenue generated by each SKU
- Stock levels by SKU
- Order quantities by SKU

This provides a product-level view of revenue and inventory-related metrics.

### Transportation Cost Analysis

Transportation costs were analyzed across four transportation modes:

- Air
- Rail
- Road
- Sea

Road transportation represented the largest share of transportation costs, followed by rail and air, while sea represented the smallest share.

### Defect Rate Analysis

Average defect rates were analyzed across product categories.

| Product Type | Average Defect Rate |
|---|---:|
| Cosmetics| 1.9 |
| Haircare | 2.5 |
| Skincare | 2.3 |

Haircare recorded the highest average defect rate among the three product categories.

Defect rates were also analyzed across transportation modes, with road transportation showing the highest share in the analysis.

- Skincare contributes the largest share of sales.

- Road transportation accounts for the largest share of transportation costs.
- Haircare has the highest average defect rate among the product categories.
